Reading it.
  • Crossing off — click a row (or focus it and hit Enter) to mark a player drafted. Struck-through rows stay legible on purpose: mid-draft you still need to check who went, and undo a misclick. Hide drafted collapses them; Reset board clears everything for a new draft.
  • Value — production rank minus draft rank, both across board members only so the scales are comparable. Bounded to the drafted pool; past that consensus stops ranking carefully and the number turns to noise.
  • Floor-Ceiling — 25th to 75th percentile of weekly scores, tick at the per-game average. Scaled within position, so bars compare RB to RB.
  • Swing — standard deviation of weekly scores. A 14 ppg player alternating 4 and 24 is a different pick from one scoring 14 every week.
  • no 2025 data — rookies. Missing rather than zero, because zero would sort them last and they are unmeasured, not bad.
Backward-looking by construction — second-year players read as overpriced because rookie usage understates a 2026 role. Consensus rank is ECR, not ADP. Production covers 2022–2025; the consensus board is a live snapshot, scraped 2026-09-04. Cross-offs are stored in this browser only.
